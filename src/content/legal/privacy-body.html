3. The information we collect

3.1 Account and identity information. When you register we collect:

  1. your CNIC number, which is your username;
  2. your first and last name as shown on your CNIC;
  3. images of the front and back of your CNIC;
  4. your mobile number in a Covered Country and your mobile number in Pakistan;
  5. your email address;
  6. records of the confirmation codes sent to your mobile numbers and email address, of your acceptance of the Terms and this Policy, and of the outcome of our manual verification of your account; and
  7. a profile picture, if you choose to add one.

The CNIC images also show the other details printed on the card, such as your photograph, date of birth, father's or husband's name, address and signature. We use those details only to verify your account and for the other purposes described in clause 4.2.

3.2 Request and Parcel information. When you create a request as a Sender we collect:

  1. your confirmation that you have read the guidance in the App, and the categories of items you select;
  2. the photos of the items unpacked with your CNIC, of the packed Parcel without the label and with the Receiver label, and of the Parcel on a weighing scale;
  3. the weight, dimensions and Declared Value of the Parcel, and the description of the items;
  4. the pickup country, city, area, Expected Pickup Range and preferred pickup time, and the delivery country, city, area and Expected Delivery Range;
  5. the amount you offer per kilogram, and each counter-offer you accept or reject; and
  6. your declaration under clause 8 of the Terms, with the date and time you accepted it.

The photo of the unpacked items shows your CNIC. The photo of the labelled Parcel shows the names, phone numbers and address written on the label.

3.3 Receiver information. The Sender gives us the Receiver's name as shown on the Receiver's CNIC, the Receiver's CNIC number and mobile number, and the delivery area and address. At delivery the Passenger uploads a photo of the Parcel with the Receiver's CNIC, which shows the details printed on that CNIC. We do not collect this information from the Receiver directly. Clause 11 explains how it is handled.

3.4 Travel information. When you offer to carry Parcels as a Passenger we collect your flight date, your countries of travel, the cities from which you can collect and to which you can deliver, your maximum spare weight, and the updates you give during travel. We do not ask for your flight number, ticket, boarding pass, passport or visa.

3.5 Collection and delivery evidence. For each Booking we collect the photo of the Parcel with the Sender's CNIC taken at collection, the photo of the Parcel with the Receiver's CNIC taken at delivery, the times at which the Parcel is marked as collected and as delivered, the Sender's confirmation or dispute of delivery, and any Problem Report with its supporting photos and documents.

3.6 Payment information. Payments are made by bank transfer. We collect the transaction references you enter in the App, the amounts, dates and status of payments, payouts and refunds, and the bank account or mobile wallet details you give us for receiving payouts and refunds. We do not collect or store debit or credit card details.

3.7 Communications and ratings. We keep the messages you send to us, and those we send to you, by email or WhatsApp, including support requests, reports about other Users and Problem Reports. We also keep the ratings that Users give each other after Completion. We do not see or record calls or messages that Users exchange directly with each other.

3.8 Device and usage information. The App uses Google Firebase and Google Analytics to measure how the App is used, and a crash reporting service to find faults. These services automatically collect information about your use of the App, such as the screens you view and the length of your sessions, and crash reports, which include technical details of your device and of the state of the App at the time of a crash. They also give each installation of the App an identifier. The analytics service may work out an approximate location, at country or city level, from a shortened form of your device's internet (IP) address.

3.9 Location. The App does not ask for access to your device's location and does not collect GPS or precise location. The places we hold are the countries, cities, areas and addresses that Users enter themselves.

3.10 We do not ask for information about racial or ethnic origin, religion, health, political opinions or sexual life. Please do not include any such information in descriptions, photos or messages.

5. The law that applies

5.1 At the date of this Policy, Pakistan has not enacted a general personal data protection law. Khepia.pk handles personal information in line with the laws in force in Pakistan, including the following provisions, each of which is in force at the date of this Policy:

  1. Article 14 of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, 1973, under which the dignity of man and, subject to law, the privacy of home are inviolable;
  2. section 41 of the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016, as amended, which makes it an offence for a person, including a service provider, who has secured access to personal information about another person while providing services under a lawful contract, to disclose it to any other person, except when required by law, without the consent of the person concerned or in breach of that contract, with intent to cause, or knowing that it is likely to cause, harm, wrongful loss or gain to any person, or to compromise the confidentiality of that information; and
  3. section 16 of the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016, as amended, which makes it an offence to obtain, sell, possess, transmit or use another person's identity information without authorisation.

5.2 If a personal data protection law comes into force in Pakistan, we will handle personal information in line with it and update this Policy.

5.3 This Policy is prepared under the laws of Pakistan. Users in a Covered Country may also have rights under the law of that country, and nothing in this Policy limits any right that cannot be limited under a law that applies to them.

6. Who we share information with

6.1 Other Users before a Booking. When a Sender's request is shared with matching Passengers, they are shown the details they need to decide whether to accept it, such as the route, the dates, the weight, the item categories and the amount offered. The Sender's personal details are not shown to a Passenger until the Sender accepts that Passenger.

6.2 Other Users after a Booking. Once a Booking is confirmed:

  1. the Passenger is shown the Sender's name and phone numbers, the pickup and delivery details, including the address on the Parcel label, the Parcel photos, and the Receiver's name, CNIC number and mobile number;
  2. the Sender is shown the Passenger's name and phone numbers; and
  3. the Sender and the Passenger can see the collection and delivery photos for that Booking.

6.3 Senders are also shown each Passenger's rating and number of completed trips, and Passengers are shown each Sender's rating, as clause 18.1 of the Terms provides.

6.4 Users must use information about other Users and Receivers only for the Booking, as clause 23.2 of the Terms requires. The Parcel label, which carries the names, phone numbers and address of the Sender and the Receiver, may also be seen by airline, security and customs staff while the Parcel is in transit.

6.5 Service providers. We use the following service providers to run the App:

  1. Google Firebase, provided by Google LLC, for the App's back-end services, which may include the storage of App data and the sending of notifications;
  2. Google Analytics, provided by Google LLC, for statistics on the use of the App;
  3. a crash reporting service, for fault reports;
  4. WhatsApp, for sending confirmation codes to mobile numbers;
  5. email service providers, for confirmation codes and messages; and
  6. banks and mobile wallet providers, for receiving payments and making payouts and refunds.

6.6 Each service provider receives only the information it needs for its service. We use service providers whose terms with us require them to keep personal information confidential and secure, to use it only for providing their service, and to give it protection equal to the protection described in this Policy. Banks, mobile wallet providers and WhatsApp also handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

6.7 Authorities. We may disclose personal information, including Booking records, photos and CNIC details, to the police, customs, courts or other authorities in Pakistan or elsewhere where we are required to do so by law or by a lawful order, or where we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to report a suspected offence, including the sending of a Prohibited Item, or to protect the safety of any person.

6.8 Disputes and legal claims. We may share Booking information with the other User concerned when dealing with a problem under clause 21.3 of the Terms, and with our lawyers and advisers where needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

6.9 Transfer of the App. If the operation of the App is transferred to another person under clause 27.4 of the Terms, personal information may be transferred to that person, who must handle it in line with this Policy. We will notify Users of the transfer.

8. How long we keep information

8.1 We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes in clause 4. We then delete it or make it anonymous. The periods are as follows:

  1. Account and identity information is kept while your account is open. After your account is closed, your CNIC images, profile picture, phone numbers, email address and payout details are deleted within 90 days, except as part of the records described in paragraphs (c) to (f).
  2. Parcel photos, collection and delivery photos, Receiver information and the details of a request are kept for 90 days after the Booking is Completed or cancelled, or after the request is withdrawn. Where a Problem Report is made, they are kept until the matter is decided under clause 21 of the Terms and for 90 days after the decision.
  3. Booking records are kept for 3 years after the Booking is Completed or cancelled. They consist of the Tracking ID, the names and CNIC numbers of the Sender and the Passenger, the route, dates, weight and Declared Value, the Sender's declaration, the status of the Booking and any decision under clause 21 of the Terms. This period follows the three years within which a suit for compensation for breach of contract may be brought under Article 115 of the First Schedule to the Limitation Act, 1908, which is in force in Pakistan.
  4. Payment records, being the transaction references, amounts and dates, and the details of the account to which each payout or refund was made, are kept for 6 years after the end of the tax year in which the payment was made. Section 174(3) of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001, which is in force in Pakistan, requires a taxpayer to keep its accounts and records for that period.
  5. Messages and support records are kept for 1 year after the matter is closed or, where they relate to a Booking or a Problem Report, for the period in paragraph (c).
  6. Where a registration is refused, the CNIC images and contact details are deleted within 30 days of the refusal. Where Khepia.pk refuses a registration, or suspends or closes an account under clause 18 of the Terms, we keep the name, the CNIC number and the reason for as long as needed to prevent fraud and to apply clause 3.1(d) of the Terms.
  7. Usage information and crash reports are kept by the analytics and crash reporting services for no longer than 14 months.

8.2 We may keep information for longer where the law, a court or an authority requires it, or where it is needed for a dispute, claim or investigation that has not been concluded. We delete it when that need ends.

8.3 Copies of deleted information may remain in secure backups for a limited period, until the backups are overwritten in the normal course. They are not used for any other purpose during that period.
