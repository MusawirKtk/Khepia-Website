17. Parcel Protection

17.1 Khepia.pk offers Senders a limited protection arrangement ("Parcel Protection"). 17.2 A Protection Payment:

  1. cannot exceed the Declared Value of the Parcel and in any case cannot exceed the maximum Declared Value shown in the App when the Booking is made;
  2. is paid only from the Deposit paid by the Passenger for that Booking and still held by Khepia.pk; and
  3. is decided by Khepia.pk at its discretion on the evidence available to it.

17.3 No Protection Payment is made where:

  1. the Problem Report was not made within the time allowed by clause 21.2, or was made after Completion;
  2. the Parcel contained a Prohibited Item or anything not declared, or any part of the Sender's declaration under clause 8 was untrue;
  3. the Parcel was detained or confiscated by customs or any other authority;
  4. the loss or damage was caused by inadequate packing or by the nature of the items; or
  5. the Sender did not upload the photos required by clause 7.2.

17.4 Parcel Protection is not insurance and Khepia.pk is not an insurer. Khepia.pk has no obligation to make any payment under Parcel Protection from its own funds. A Protection Payment is not an admission of liability by Khepia.pk. Parcel Protection is separate from Khepia.pk's own liability, and a Protection Payment does not count towards the limit in clause 19.4.

17.5 A Sender who accepts a Protection Payment accepts it in full settlement of the Sender's claims against Khepia.pk in respect of that Parcel.
